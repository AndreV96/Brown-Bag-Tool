What is a Brown Bag?

A Brown Bag is an informal, lunch-time session where someone on the team shares something they know — a technical deep dive, a lesson learned, a tool they've been using, or a topic they're simply curious about. The name comes from bringing your own lunch while you watch. It's a low-pressure way to spread knowledge across the team without requiring a big time investment or a polished production.

Format & Characteristics

Duration
45–60 minutes (30–45 min talk + 10–15 min Q&A)
Format
Live presentation with Q&A — in-person, over Zoom, or hybrid
Frequency
Roughly weekly, scheduled over the lunch hour
Audience
Open to anyone in the org; attendance is optional
Recording
Sessions are recorded when possible so people who can't attend live can catch up afterward

How to Prepare

  1. Pick a topic. Anything you know well or are excited to learn out loud about counts.
  2. Keep it informal. A few slides or a live demo is enough — it doesn't need to be a polished deck.
  3. Structure your time. A short intro, the main content, a live example if you have one, and a wrap-up.
  4. Leave room for questions. Aim to finish your material with 10–15 minutes to spare.
  5. Share your resources. Slides, repos, or docs you reference get added to the session's page afterward so others can revisit them.
  6. Test your setup. If presenting remotely, check your screen share and audio a few minutes before the session starts.

Schedule a Presentation

Scheduling, rescheduling, and canceling Brown Bag sessions happens in Slack now — not here. Here's how:

  1. In Slack, run /brownbag schedule.
  2. Fill in the title and description, and pick a date from the list of currently open slots.
  3. Submit — you're on the calendar right away, with no risk of double-booking.

To reschedule or cancel your own session, run /brownbag manage and select it. To see upcoming and past sessions from Slack, run /brownbag list.
